# FinFolio — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 1, 2026
**Last updated:** March 2026

---

## Overview

FinFolio is a personal finance app designed to help you plan goals, track investments, and manage your financial life. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, what we do not collect, and how your information is handled.

---

## Your Financial Data

**We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any of your personal financial data.**

All financial information you enter in FinFolio — including goals, investment amounts, portfolio values, transaction history, and retirement projections — is stored exclusively on your device as a local database file. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server, including ours.

---

## What We Collect

FinFolio uses the following third-party services which collect limited, anonymous data:

### Firebase Crashlytics
We use Firebase Crashlytics (by Google) to collect anonymous crash reports. When the app crashes, a report is sent that includes:
- The type of error that occurred
- Device model and Android version
- App version number

Crash reports do **not** contain any personal information, financial data, or any content you have entered in the app.

### Firebase Analytics
We use Firebase Analytics (by Google) to collect anonymous usage data. This includes:
- Which features are used (e.g. goal created, snapshot added)
- Session length and frequency
- Device type, OS version, and general region

Analytics data does **not** contain any personal information or financial data. No individually identifiable information is collected.

You can review Google's privacy policy at: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

---

## What We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information
- We do not collect any financial account numbers, PAN, Aadhaar, or identity documents
- We do not collect your investment values, goal amounts, or any financial figures you enter
- We do not serve ads
- We do not sell data to third parties
- We do not require account registration

---

## Data Storage and Security

All app data is stored locally on your device. You are responsible for keeping your device secure. FinFolio provides a backup feature that lets you export your data to Google Drive or any service of your choice — this is entirely at your discretion and under your control.

---

## Children's Privacy

FinFolio is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
